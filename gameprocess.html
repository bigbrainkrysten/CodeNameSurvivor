How Survivor Works

Outwit. Outplay. Outlast. Learn every stage of the world's greatest social experiment.

Overview

Survivor places contestants in remote locations where they must rely on teamwork, strategy, endurance, and social relationships to remain in the game. Throughout the competition, players participate in challenges, vote each other out at Tribal Council, and ultimately compete for the title of Sole Survivor and a one million dollar prize.

1. The Tribes Begin

The game begins with contestants divided into tribes. Each tribe establishes a camp, builds shelter, gathers food, and works together to survive harsh conditions while forming alliances that may determine their future in the game.

2. Reward Challenges

Reward Challenges provide food, supplies, and comfort items that improve life around camp. Winning rewards can strengthen tribe morale while creating opportunities for strategic conversations and alliance building.

3. Immunity Challenges

Immunity Challenges determine which tribe—or later, which individual—is safe from elimination. The losing players must prepare for Tribal Council, where someone will be voted out of the game.

4. Tribal Council

At Tribal Council, contestants discuss recent events before secretly voting to eliminate one player. Jeff Probst reveals each vote until one contestant hears the famous phrase, "The tribe has spoken."

5. The Merge

When enough contestants remain, the tribes merge into one. Individual gameplay becomes the focus, making strategy, trust, and timing even more important.

6. The Jury

Players eliminated after the merge become members of the jury. They observe the remaining contestants and later determine who deserves to win the game.

7. Final Tribal Council

The finalists explain their gameplay to the jury by defending their strategic, physical, and social decisions. Jurors ask questions before casting their votes.

8. Sole Survivor

The contestant receiving the most jury votes becomes the Sole Survivor and wins the one million dollar grand prize, completing one of television's most demanding competitions.

Keys to Winning Survivor

Outwit

Develop strong alliances while staying one step ahead strategically.

Outplay

Win challenges when immunity matters most.

Outlast

Manage relationships and survive every Tribal Council until the very end.
